# Code of conduct

PosturaNet is built in the open, with people who run cloud security for a living, people
learning it, and AI agents working for both. Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other PosturaNet space agrees to this.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be specific and constructive in reviews: say what is wrong and what would fix it.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.
- Keep real customer data out of public spaces: use synthetic findings, addresses and
  account ids (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Posting exploit details for an unfixed vulnerability in public. Report it privately as
  described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by email to **founderposturanet@gmail.com** with "conduct" in the subject.
Reports are handled privately, and we reply within 3 business days.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this
code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does. Decisions are made
after hearing from the people involved, when that is safe.

This applies in all PosturaNet spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.
